What we offer

Care for every stage of life

A single hospital record follows the patient from the first consultation to the last follow-up, so nothing gets lost between departments.

Outpatient consultations

Search a specialist by name or specialization and book a slot from their next seven days of availability.

Appointment management

Reschedule or cancel without a phone call. A cancelled slot is released immediately for other patients.

Medical history

Diagnosis, prescriptions and treatment plans recorded by the doctor and available to the patient afterwards.

Departments

Cardiology, Orthopaedics, Paediatrics, Dermatology and more, each with its own specialists and rooms.

Role-based access

Administrators, doctors and patients each get their own portal and can only reach the records that belong to them.

Hospital overview

Live counts of doctors, patients and appointments so the front desk always knows what the day looks like.
